Blackjack


Core Features
-Dealing Cards
-Identifying win/lose conditions
    -All players bust = Lose
    -Player has higher score than Dealer = Win
    -Dealer busts while players remain = Win
-Handling Player actions
    -Hit
        -Take another card
    -Stay
        -Keep current cards and end turn
    -Double Down (Optional)
        -Doubles Bet, takes one final card, then stays
    -Split (Optional)
        -If cards are a matching pair
            -Split into two hands
            -Place a second equal bet on the new hand
-Handling the value of Aces

Game Rules
-52 Cards
-Card values
    -Face Card = 10
    -Ace = 1 or 11
    -Number Cards = Number
-Game Progression
    -Initial bets are placed
    -Cards are dealt
        -Players receive two cards face-up
        -Dealer receives one card face-up and one face-Down
    -Player Turn Actions
        -Hit or Split to continue play
        -Double Down or Stay to end turn
    -Dealer Turn
        -Reveal face down card
        -Dealer must Hit until reaching 17 or higher
        -Dealer busts by going over 21 
            -All remaining players 21 and under win
    -Betting Payouts
        -Normal Win paid 1:1
        -Blackjack (Ace + value 10) paid 3:2
        -Push (tie) paid bet returned
        -Lose dealer collects bet
